How it works

From a missed call to a closed incident.

Six steps, most of them automatic. The caller never has to explain where they are.

  1. Caller · any phone

    Calls or texts the Kaaka number

    A plain call, or an SMS with HELP, SOS or സഹായം for someone who cannot talk.

  2. Kaaka number

    Rejects the call, replies by SMS

    Free for the caller. The reply says help is being arranged and, in immediate danger, to call 112.

  3. Caller's phone

    The reply starts the location beacon

    With Kaaka Sensors installed, GPS reports flow back automatically. Without it, a link lets them share location from the browser.

  4. Responders

    The nearest responder is dispatched

    Distance and ETA by SMS, with a map link and “Reply ACK to confirm”. Nobody answers in five minutes? A reminder goes out.

  5. Control room

    One incident, on a live map

    Calls, help texts, alerts and the caller's route are grouped per caller, with the nearest responder drawn on the map.

  6. Caller or responder

    Closed when help arrives

    A responder texts RESOLVE, or the caller texts SAFE. Everything stays on record for later.

What you get

Three small parts. One help line.

Gateway app

The Kaaka number

↗

An Android phone that screens every incoming call, including saved contacts, replies by SMS, texts responders and reports to the control room. Works with the screen locked.

Caller app

Kaaka Sensors

↗

An SOS button, fall detection, a safety timer and an “I'm safe” button. Location reports go out even when Android won't let the app open itself.

Dashboard

The control room

↗

Live incidents and routes, history with search, analytics, a responder registry and desktop notifications. Runs on any laptop.

Safety by design

Built for the worst moment, not the best one.

Works without data

Plain calls and SMS

Basic phones, weak signal, no balance for data. A rejected call costs the caller nothing.

Location stays private

Only the Kaaka number can start it

The caller's beacon starts only for a reply from their configured Kaaka number. A stranger cannot request it.

Protected dashboard

Locked before it goes public

The server refuses a public address without an admin token, and replies are rate-limited per caller.

Alongside 112

Never instead of it

Every reply points to 112 for immediate danger. Kaaka adds location, tracking and a responder loop.

For campuses, colonies and families

Run a help line with one phone.

Kaaka is open source. Set up the Kaaka number, add your responders, and open the control room on any laptop.
